# Cannery Line

A one-button factory game. Food rides the belts into each line's can. Tap to push rotten or stray food into the bin before it spoils a can.

- **Levels:** nine levels, from one line up to three.
- **Battle:** two players side by side on the same item chart. Left player uses A, right player uses L, or tap your own half of the screen. Choose 1 to 3 lines and 30, 45 or 60 seconds.
- **Controls:** Space or tap to push, P or Esc to pause.

Open `index.html` through any static host to play.
